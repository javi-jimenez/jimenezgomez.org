This page explains how this site uses cookies and how you can manage them.

Essential cookies: necessary for the site to function.

Analytics cookies: we use Google Analytics to collect aggregated and anonymous usage data. Any analytics script is loaded only if you give your explicit consent.

Consent management:

- The site displays a banner allowing you to Accept, Reject, or Configure preferences.
- The decision is saved in the browser's local storage (`localStorage`) and you can change it at any time using the "Cookies" button on the page.
- The site does not load Google Analytics or other measurement scripts without your consent.

Contact: for privacy inquiries, contact the site manager.
