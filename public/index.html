Home Telecare (2003-2005)

Main developer of the home telecare system deployed by the Generalitat Valenciana: a base unit plugged into the telephone line, a constant presence sensor and a central alarm, plus a pendant or pocket button that, when pressed, calls the emergency centre — the same kind of device that Cruz Roja and the regional government hand out to elderly people living alone. Everything ran on a Linux set-top box. Built at the TSB group of ITACA, Universitat Politècnica de València, within the EU CONFIDENT framework project.

Java Linux Set-top-box X10 Telemedicine EU Project
🏛️ ITACA · UPV
